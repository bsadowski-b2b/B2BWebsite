How B to B-Link works

Integrated EDI that reduces rekeying and exception work.

B to B-Link exists as an add-on module inside Sage 100, so documents can move through familiar workflows with validation, history, scheduling, and secure AS2 communications.

  1. Receive and validate documents Bring partner requirements into a controlled workflow before transactions reach Sage 100.
  2. Process inside Sage 100 Turn orders, invoices, ASNs, and acknowledgments into usable business transactions.
  3. Ship, label, and report Use logs, audit trails, error trapping, validation, scheduling, and filtering tools.
